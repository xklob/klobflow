Continuous streak power and much brighter glow

- Streak glow grows continuously with earned alignment time, reaching MAX POWER at 30 seconds. The HUD shows glow percentage instead of 2/5/10-second levels, and elapsed and best streak times keep counting beyond maximum power.
- Maximum power adds a much larger white-gold aura, luminous staff wake, smooth energy rays, and orbiting sparks. Guides, measured tip rings, and correction labels remain above the effects. Android and browser practice use matching visuals.
- Reduced-motion settings freeze accent rotation. Effects remain optional. Existing tracking and alignment forgiveness preserve earned power through brief interruptions; uncertain or hidden tips never gain streak time or an aura.

Validation and publication checks are recorded in docs/validation.md. This update changes streak progression and presentation, not the detector or precision measurements.

The signed APK retains package com.caleb.klobflow and the existing signing certificate, with versionCode 13. Update through Klobstore or install the APK from this release. The companion ZIP supports browser-only practice and Android streaming; update it and refresh the browser.
